Why Workbase MeetBuilt for Nigerian internet

1Data saver and live data meter

Attendees see exactly how many MB the event is using. With one tap, data saver keeps the sound and slides and drops video to save data.

2Reconnects by itself

When an attendee's network drops, the session reconnects automatically. Nobody has to find the link again.

3No app to download

Attendees join from a link on any phone or laptop browser, by typing their name. No account is needed.

4Host control

Waiting room, lock the room, mute everyone, remove participants, stop a screen share and bring people on stage.

On the dayHow the event would run

  1. Before doors openSpeakers join a backstage check 30 minutes early with our team: sound, camera, slides.
  2. Doors openAttendees join from the event link. A recording notice is shown before they enter if the session is recorded.
  3. SessionsSpeakers present with screen sharing. The audience reacts, chats and raises hands; the host brings people up for questions.
  4. After the eventYour team downloads the attendance record and keeps the recording. We share a short report of how the event ran.

Privacy and securityNothing kept that you didn't ask for

  • All audio and video are encrypted in transit and carried on Cloudflare's global network.
  • Event chat is deleted the moment the event ends. The meeting record is deleted 7 days later.
  • Recordings are never uploaded to us. They are saved only on the recording device.
  • The attendance record is available only to the event host.
  • We are happy to sign a confidentiality agreement or data processing agreement and to answer your security team's questions.
