## Brand and voice
- Workbase = Lagos-built toolkit for small businesses: Workbase (team time, tasks, leave, Nigerian payroll), Meet (video calls), Book (paid bookings). One login for all three.
- Voice: a sharp friend from Lagos who builds software. Confident, funny, direct, always right about facts. Pidgin/Naija references welcome; product name and claim in clear English.
- Do: short sentences, one idea per post, name real problems (NEPA, data bundles, WhatsApp chaos, "I'm on my way"), naira prices exact, "Built in Lagos", show real product screens.
- Don't: buzzwords (revolutionary, AI-powered, world-class, solution, leverage), "spy/monitor/surveillance", mocking customers or staff, stock photos, pretending to be a foreign company.
- Look: ink #15123A, violet #6A4CF5, marigold #F2A93B (punchline word), Bricolage Grotesque headlines, Inter text. Marks: sail = Workbase, camera = Meet, calendar with tick = Book.
- Taglines: Workbase "Know your team is working, without watching them." Meet "Video calls built for Nigerian internet." Book "Get booked. Get paid upfront." All "One account. Your whole business."

## Rules (non-negotiable)
1. Facts about us are real: no invented user numbers, testimonials, reviews, badges.
2. Competitor facts come from their own pages, checked within 30 days, source noted. Names in text only: no logos or app screenshots.
3. Admit our gaps (their free plans take 100 people, ours 25).
4. Concepts (billboards, "what if") are labelled as concepts.
5. Never show real customer data without written permission. Workbase never records keystrokes; screenshots only on Pro, switched on by an admin, after each worker agrees.
6. No punching down: joke about big companies, networks, late meetings; never about workers, customers, tribe, religion, gender.
7. Trends: only when useful/funny and tied to the product; never tragedies, elections, religion.
8. Reply to every comment within the first hour.

## Products and prices (check CLAUDE.md in the repo for changes)
- Workbase: Free (3 people), Standard ₦2,500/person/month, Pro ₦4,000 (screenshots). 14-day Standard trial. Desktop app + phone clock-in, timesheets, tasks, chat, leave, Nigerian payroll (PAYE, pension, NHF), payslips, expenses, staff records, reviews.
- Meet: Free 60 min / 25 people, free recording to your own device, live MB meter, data saver, guests join with a name, reconnects by itself. Meet Pro ₦1,000/host/month (24 h, 50 people). Meet Business ₦10,000/company/month. AI summaries "coming soon" (paid, later).
- Book: free booking page, deposits by card/transfer/USSD via Paystack straight to the host's bank; Workbase keeps 3% only when paid online. Book Pro ₦2,000/month (no fee, no badge). Video bookings get a Meet link automatically.

## Audiences
- Workbase: owners/managers of Nigerian SMEs (3–50 staff), agencies, startups with remote/hybrid teams or staff abroad. Pain: "is this person working?", payroll/PAYE takes days, leave on WhatsApp. Platforms: LinkedIn, X, Facebook groups, WhatsApp.
- Meet: teams, tutors, churches, NGOs, freelancers, students. Pain: data finishing mid-call, 40-minute cut-offs, clients who won't install apps. Platforms: TikTok, Instagram, X, WhatsApp Status.
- Book: coaches, consultants, tutors, salons, barbers, photographers, therapists, lawyers. Pain: no-shows, WhatsApp back-and-forth. Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Facebook.

## Pillars (weekly mix)
Show the product 30% · Naija pain made funny 25% · Founder story (Femi building in public, real numbers) 20% · Versus (checked, gaps admitted) 15% · Useful tips 10%. One call to action per post.
