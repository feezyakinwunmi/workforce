## LinkedIn (carousel or the 16:9 film)

I built something for every Nigerian business with people working from home, from another city or from another country.

It's called Workbase. It answers the question every remote-team owner asks at 9am: "Is anyone actually working?"

→ Live view of who's working, idle or on their phone, in each person's own time zone
→ Tasks with time logged and a "what I got done" report at clock-out
→ Clock in from the desktop app or any phone, with a reminder that rings at start time
→ Team chat, leave and Nigerian public holidays
→ Payroll with PAYE (Nigeria Tax Act 2025 bands), pension and NHF, and PDF payslips by email
→ Expense claims, staff records, goals and reviews

It tracks work, not people. Nothing runs before clock-in, keystrokes are never recorded, and there's no webcam or microphone. Everyone can see their own data.

Free for up to 3 people. ₦2,500 per person per month after that, with a 14-day free trial and no card needed.

Built in Lagos, for teams everywhere. I'd love your feedback, and if you run a remote team, try it this week 👇
