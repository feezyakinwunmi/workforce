## The facts behind every claim (checked 16 Oct 2026)

- **Google Meet, free account:** group calls up to 60 minutes, up to 100 people. Recording is only for paid Google Workspace plans (Business Standard and up) and Google One 2 TB+. Source: support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681
- **Zoom Basic (free):** group calls end at 40 minutes, up to 100 people, local recording from the desktop app only, no cloud recording. Pro is about $13–14 a month on yearly billing ($16.99 monthly). Sources: Zoom pricing summaries (usecarly.com/blog/zoom-pricing, ionos.com guide to recording Zoom)
- **Zoho Meeting, free:** 60 minutes, up to 100 people, no recording. Source: zeeg.me Zoho Meeting review (2026)
- **Workbase Meet, free:** 60 minutes, 25 people, recording free from the browser (phone or laptop), live MB meter, data saver. Meet Pro ₦1,000/month: 24 hours, 50 people.

If anyone says "but they take 100 people": they're right. Say so, and say bigger meetings are coming. That honesty is the post.

Re-check the facts before posting again in a few months: plans change.
